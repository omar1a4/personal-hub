# Development Guide

- Read [PROJECT_BRIEF.md](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) before starting work. It is the source of truth for the product vision, current milestone, privacy requirements, and open decisions.
- Keep the brief updated as agreed decisions, scope, and implementation status evolve. Record significant decisions with a date and short rationale; distinguish decisions from proposals.
- Work in manageable stages and explain actions briefly in plain language. The user is learning to direct AI through VS Code. Avoid overengineering and keep later goals out of the current milestone unless explicitly agreed.
- Follow the teaching preferences: concise explanations, many concrete examples, step-by-step reasoning, and preservation of the user's own understanding. Do not reveal exercise solutions unless explicitly requested.
- Accurately distinguish working, verified features from planned features and mockups. Label sample content and any mock chat clearly; report what was tested and what remains unverified.
- Education pages are publicly readable without sign-in. Health, sleep and dreams, spirituality, and personal reflections are accessible only to the authorized owner account; signing up grants no access to the owner's private areas.
- Only the owner may access the assistant, edit content, upload material, or instruct the assistant to change the website. Enforce these permissions and access to private content, including images and files, on the backend; signing in alone does not establish owner authorization.
- Keep assistant conversations and personal learning records private unless the owner explicitly publishes selected content. Public lectures and published breakthrough annotations must remain separate from private chat history.
- The first working milestone requires a real official account connection and completed AI response, lecture context, and an owner-reviewed annotation that can be applied, persists across refresh, and can be undone. Mock chat does not satisfy it. Distinguish real local owner checks from unimplemented hosted authentication and placeholder private areas.
- Follow the brief's official Sign in with ChatGPT plan-usage path. Verify eligibility, consent, and completed inference; do not equate identity sign-in with plan usage or local eligibility with hosted-app approval. Do not switch to separately billed API usage or change spending settings without discussing it with the owner and obtaining approval.
- Keep private content and credentials out of public repositories. Keep OAuth tokens in protected local backend storage outside Git, never in the browser or logs; API credentials also remain backend-only. Resolve publishing and hosted-integration access decisions before deployment.
- The development agent builds the product; the embedded assistant acts through explicitly implemented application tools. Do not imply it inherits ChatGPT account context or memories.
- Current authorization (2026-11-20): implement daily study and sleep/dream journals, maintain the real local assistant, and create/update the GitHub repository and publish the course demo. Course development is paused. Keep the private backend loopback-only; the public demo contains only fictional data and clearly states that live AI is local. Preserve the existing Technion Copilot connection and billing settings. See README.md for startup and PROJECT_BRIEF.md for current verification limits.
